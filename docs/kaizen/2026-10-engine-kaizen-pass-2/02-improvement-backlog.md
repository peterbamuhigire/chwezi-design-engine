## Root causes (5 Whys, critical and repeated defects)

**H-1 and the path family (H-2, H-3, H-4).** Why is a user name and an unlicensed-copy file name public? The source register was written to record which local books informed a skill. Why with local paths? It was authored on one machine from the file explorer and committed as written. Why was that not caught? The 29 October check for machine paths was a hand search limited to active skill files, and no validator scans routers, integration records, hooks or registers. Why only skills? The prior backlog item (D-4) was scoped by the files that were then known to matter. Why no validator? Standardising a hand check into a gate was listed in the prompt's Step 6 but not done for paths. *Root cause: tooling gap (no portability gate), plus a process gap (a hand check was not promoted).*

**T1-1 and T1-2 (token example).** Why does the example contradict the edition? On 29 October the register and skill were updated to name 2025.10; the example was not re-read against that edition. Why? The validator checks structure and aliases only and says so, so a green run implied nothing about shape. Why are the contrast figures wrong? They were typed into `$description` strings and nothing recomputes them. *Root cause: proof gap (claims in prose with no check), compounded by a currency edit made without re-validating the artefacts it governs.*

**T2-1 and T3-1 (detector and font gate).** Why do the machine checks miss four of six seeded defects and every PostScript font name? Both were built from the doctrine's text: rules exist where the doctrine states a CSS-visible pattern, and the font matcher reads CSS family names. Why not target size or rendered fonts? Neither has a registry row or a script, and the verification skill describes them as manual steps. *Root cause: tooling (scanner coverage), not skill text.*

**X-1.** The validator assumes sibling folders named like repositories. Repositories were renamed upstream and local folders were not. *Root cause: tooling (hard-coded layout).*
