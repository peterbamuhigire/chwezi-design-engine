## Source

`Chwezi_Core_Systems_Design_Studio_Bible_2026` (CCS-DSB-001, edition 1.0, 8 November 2026, a proposed
corporate design constitution; about 151,000 words in 11 parts). Read in waves: Parts I-VI in full
(constitution, foundations, architecture and interoperability, product experience, the four
companions, delivery governance); Part VII-IX by heading with Annexes G, H and M and Contract 26
read in full; Part X (the universal UI/UX atlas) and Part XI (the four companion manuals) compared
by heading, because they are the source texts of the standards already adopted.

## What the bible confirms

The structure we adopted: one parent (CDS) and four companions (C1 dashboards and analytics, C2
documents and publishing, C3 presentations and slide decks, C4 infographics and visual communication);
the ten invariants (truth before beauty, no AI-slop lettering, illustrative not synthetic imagery,
accessibility by construction, fast on modest devices, one content model many outputs, editable
source, no ornament without purpose, protected workflows, measured quality); the authority ladder
(law and client, safety and finance, parent, companion, product patterns); the density modes and
width classes already in Product Excellence.

## Decisions for the owner (not changed)

1. **Review weights.** The bible proposes factual truth 22, task success 20, accessibility 18,
   performance 15, visual craft 15, delivery 10. Product Excellence has its own six weighted
   dimensions and each child its own score. Replacing any is a major version under change control.
2. **Bible Part VIII (36 component and media contracts)** and **Annex G (20 cross-medium
   transformation recipes)** are strong candidates for fixtures and component specifications; they are
   large and belong to a dedicated wave.
3. **Annex I (domain applicability profiles)** and **Annex K (agent prompt library)** could become
   references of the matching skills.
4. **Annex H** device matrix rows not yet explicit in Product Excellence: browser at 400% zoom
   (320 CSS px reflow, already inside the WCAG 2.2 AA gate), foldable and split-screen resizing, and
   four-hour offline replay; the existing gates cover them in substance.
